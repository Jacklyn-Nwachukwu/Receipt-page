Jollof Rice

Recipe by Jacklyn Nwachukwu.

The difference between weekday jollof and the kind served in a rented canopy at four in the afternoon is patience. The pepper base is cooked down until it turns the colour of rust, the rice steams without being stirred, and the last five minutes over higher heat are what give it that smoke. Nothing here is difficult. It just refuses to be rushed.
